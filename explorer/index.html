Crime capture explorer

Given a target land area, find the areas that capture the most police.uk recorded crime of a chosen type — per police force, spatial unit and time window. All computation happens in your browser; every query runs live against Azure Blob Storage over short-lived, read-only SAS tokens — nothing is pre-aggregated or cached server-side.

Hover a highlighted area for its crime count; the red outline reports the force-wide total. Yellow areas are the densest "captured" set within the coverage target; blue (optional) contain crimes that didn't make the cut.
